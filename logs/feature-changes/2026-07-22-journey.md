## Notable decisions

- **Center-spine alternating** (owner pick over PLAN's left rail; left rail
  remains as the <lg collapse). Entries `lg:w-[calc(50%-3.5rem)]`,
  `self-start`/`self-end` alternating, text edges toward the spine.
- Rail = one normalized `d` (amplitude ±20 of layer width) scaled by
  `scalePathD` into a px-space 1:1 viewBox (04 dash-fragmentation gotcha);
  the same d reads ±32px on the lg center spine (w-40 layer) and ±8px on the
  mobile left strip (w-10).
- **Award hover-invert lives on an INNER wrapper**, not the li — the
  `-m-4/p-4` hover padding on the li shifted its border box and pulled the
  spine dots off-axis (caught in the first capture; dots verified at exactly
  container center for all 9 after the fix).
- Dots are kind-coded color as ENHANCEMENT only — kind is already carried by
  the text (employmentType chip, org, award titles); no color-only meaning.
- sr-only `h2` "Journey" before the `h3` titles — avoids repeating ch.04's
  Lighthouse heading-order carry.
- Stack chips are quiet hairline text pills (`border-line font-mono
  text-meta`), NOT 04's glass badges — Jira/Docs have no icons; metadata, not
  showcase.
- Award rows are NOT focusable — the invert is a pure hover echo; no content
  is hover-gated.
