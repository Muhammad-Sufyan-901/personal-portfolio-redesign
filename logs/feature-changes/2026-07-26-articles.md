## Notable decisions

- **Chapter index `09`; Contact moves to `10`.** As-built eyebrows run 02, 03, 04, 06, 07, 08 with `05` free from the pre-reorder numbering, but Articles sits *after* Journey in the DOM, so reusing 05 would break ascending narrative order. Agent memory previously reserved 09 for Contact; that is now 10.
- **Travel is measured, never tuned** — `track.scrollWidth − innerWidth`. Adding an article re-lengthens the pin with no number to touch.
- **Degenerate-rail guard.** With few enough articles the track already fits and pinning would eat a viewport to move zero pixels. A `ResizeObserver` sets `fitsInView`, which falls through to the same flow layout reduced motion uses. Rejected alternative: pin unconditionally and accept a dead viewport at ≤2 articles.
- **Card height is rail-driven, not aspect-driven.** First pass used `aspect-[4/5]` covers; at 1440×900 every card overflowed and was clipped mid-sentence, and the coverless card floated out of line. Now the row is `min-h-0 flex-1` and cards are `h-full`, so the cover absorbs leftover height — plus `max-h-[34rem]` on the track, because `h-full` alone grew an 800px cover on a 1024-tall viewport.
- **The cover frame renders even without a cover** — a blank `raised` plate is the system's own tonal step and keeps every card's text block on one baseline. Rejected: omitting the frame (cards fell out of alignment).
- **Progress meter is 2px, not the 1px hairline.** At 1px the chapter's single ember moment washed out to grey on screen. The Hairline Depth Rule governs borders; this is a meter.
- **Minimal card (owner revision, same day).** First pass carried a three-line clamped standfirst under the title; the owner asked for every card to match the minimal one, so the card is now cover → meta → title → Read. `summary` was removed from the `Article` interface and the stubs rather than left unrendered — a field nobody displays is one the owner would fill for nothing. Covers stay (owner is supplying files); the title keeps its `line-clamp-3` safety valve.
- **Reused, not reinvented:** Gallery's pin + exp-damp applier on the shared `gsap.ticker`; Journey's word-span de-veil statement grammar (`RevealText` can't wrap nested emphasis spans); `Link`'s automatic external `rel`/`target`; `data-cursor` label pill.
